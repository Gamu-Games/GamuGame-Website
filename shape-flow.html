Shape Flow

Shape Flow is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by CÔNG TY TNHH GAMU GAME STUDIOS. Drop blocks into the Crusher, turn them into a rainbow of tiny candies and sort them into matching trays before the conveyor belt overflows.

GET IT ONGoogle Play
Android Puzzle / Casual Rated for 3+
How to play

Tap. Crush. Sort. Repeat.

  1. 1

    Tap to drop

    Tap a colorful block in the top container and watch it fall into the Crusher.

  2. 2

    Crush & create

    The Crusher grinds each block into hundreds of tiny candies of the same color.

  3. 3

    Sort by color

    Candies ride the conveyor belt into the tray that matches their color. Fill a tray to clear it.

  4. 4

    Don’t overload

    The belt has limited space. Crush the right colors at the right time, or it’s game over.

Features

Simple to play. Oddly satisfying.

Satisfying mechanics

Watch large blocks get crushed into fluid, physics-based candy particles.

Strategic puzzles

Prioritize which blocks to drop based on the trays waiting at the end of the belt.

Challenging blockers

Obstacles and hidden blocks keep every level fresh as you advance.
