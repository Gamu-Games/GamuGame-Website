Who we are

A focused studio building games that last.

This website is the official online presence of CÔNG TY TNHH GAMU GAME STUDIOS, a limited liability company registered in Hanoi, Vietnam. We design, develop and publish digital games, with a current focus on mobile platforms.

Our work covers gameplay design, Unity development, live operations, user acquisition integration and product iteration.

Company information

Player-first

Every decision starts with how a game feels in the player’s hands: simple to learn, satisfying to master.

Quality over quantity

We ship fewer titles and polish them further, from first prototype to live operations.

Data-informed iteration

We measure, learn and improve continuously based on gameplay quality, usability and retention.
